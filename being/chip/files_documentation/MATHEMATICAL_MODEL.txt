                    1. FMA: FORCE OF MASS PER A CUBIC
================================================================================

1.1 DEFINITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FMA = Force of Mass per unit cubic volume

    FMA = F / V

    Where:
    F = Force (Newtons)
    V = Volume (cubic meters, m³)

1.2 ENERGY DENSITY CALCULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Energy density derived from FMA:

    E_density = FMA × c²

    Where:
    c = speed of light (299,792,458 m/s)

2.1 HAWKING RADIATION PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Black hole radiation temperature:

    T_H = (ℏ × c³) / (8π × G × M × k_B)

    Where:
    ℏ = reduced Planck constant (1.0545718 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s)
    c = speed of light
    G = gravitational constant (6.67430 × 10⁻¹¹ m³/kg·s²)
    M = mass of the black hole (kg)
    k_B = Boltzmann constant (1.380649 × 10⁻²³ J/K)

2.2 ENERGY COUPLING EQUATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    E_coupled = T_external × T_internal × σ × A

    T_external = external temperature (atmospheric void - cold state)
    T_internal = internal Hawking radiation temperature
    σ = Stefan-Boltzmann constant (5.670374419 × 10⁻⁸ W/m²K⁴)
