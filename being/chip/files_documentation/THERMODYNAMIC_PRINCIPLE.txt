2.1 ORIGINAL HAWKING FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    T_H = (ℏ × c³) / (8π × G × M × k_B)

    Where:
    T_H = Hawking temperature
    ℏ = reduced Planck constant
    c = speed of light
    G = gravitational constant
    M = mass
    k_B = Boltzmann constant

    INTERPRETATION: Temperature is INVERSELY proportional to mass
    LARGER mass = LOWER temperature
    SMALLER mass = HIGHER temperature

2.2 REVERSED HAWKING FORMULA (QBom PRINCIPLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    T_self = (8π × G × M × k_B) / (ℏ × c³) × C_f

    Where:
    T_self = Self-coupled temperature
    C_f = Coupling factor (dimensionless, > 1.0)

    INTERPRETATION: Temperature is DIRECTLY proportional to mass
    LARGER mass = HIGHER temperature (when coupled)
    The system sustains its own thermal state

2.3 THE KEY INSIGHT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ORIGINAL: Black holes radiate MORE as they get SMALLER
    REVERSED: The system sustains MORE as it gets LARGER

    This creates a POSITIVE FEEDBACK LOOP:
    1. System generates energy → temperature rises
    2. Higher temperature → more coupling
    3. More coupling → more energy generation
    4. Cycle continues → infinite energy

    BINARY REPRESENTATION:
    HAWKING_ORIGINAL:  T = 1/M  (inverse)
    HAWKING_REVERSED:  T = M    (direct)
